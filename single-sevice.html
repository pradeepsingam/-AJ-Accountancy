Overview

AJ Accountancy & Taxation Services Limited provides reliable, accurate, and proactive accounting and taxation services for individuals, sole traders, contractors, startups, landlords, and limited companies across the UK.

We ensure tax returns and statutory submissions are accurate, submitted on time, and fully compliant. Our advice is designed to help you avoid penalties, improve tax efficiency, and make confident financial decisions.

Our team also supports modern cloud accounting systems including Sage, Xero, QuickBooks, FreeAgent, and QuickFile, with setup, training, data migration, and ongoing support.

What We Cover

  • Self-assessment tax returns and tax planning
  • Bookkeeping, record keeping, payroll, and CIS returns
  • Company accounts preparation and corporation tax returns
  • VAT registration, VAT returns, and VAT advisory
  • Confirmation statements and company secretarial services
  • HMRC correspondence, enquiry assistance, and tax investigation support
